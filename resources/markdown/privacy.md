# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27th December 2025_

## Plain English Summary

- This website collects **only the data it needs to work properly**
- Any personal data you provide is used **only for the reason you gave it**
- Your data is **not sold or used for advertising**
- You have legal rights over your data, including asking for it to be deleted
- This site follows UK GDPR principles: fairness, transparency, and minimal collection

The detailed policy below explains this more formally.

---

## 1. Who We Are

This website is operated by **Sharif Khan**.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, we are the **data controller**. This means we decide how and why your personal data is processed.

**Contact email:** support@stupidly.uk

---

## 2. What Personal Data We Collect

### a. Data You Provide Directly

We collect personal data when you voluntarily provide it, for example by:
- Sending a message via a contact form
- Providing feedback or enquiries

This may include:
- Name
- Email address
- Message content
- Any information you choose to include

You are not required to provide more information than necessary.

### b. Data Collected Automatically

When you visit the website, we may collect limited technical information, including:
- IP address
- Browser and device type
- Pages visited and time spent on them
- Referring website

This data is used for technical operation, security, and basic understanding of site usage.

---

## 3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we must have a lawful reason to process personal data. We rely on the following bases:

- **Consent**  
  When you choose to submit personal data (for example, sending a message)

- **Legitimate interests**  
  To operate, secure, and improve the website, where this does not override your rights and freedoms

- **Legal obligation**  
  Where processing is required to comply with the law

---

## 4. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to:
- Operate and maintain the website
- Respond to enquiries or messages
- Improve site performance and reliability
- Maintain security and prevent misuse

We do not use personal data for:
- Advertising
- Profiling
- Automated decision-making

---

## 5. Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to:
- Ensure the site functions correctly
- Understand how the site is used

Where required, consent will be requested before non-essential cookies are set.

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

---

## 6. Sharing Personal Data

Personal data is **not sold**.

Data may be shared only:
- With service providers (such as hosting or analytics providers) acting on our instructions
- Where required by law or legal process
- To protect the rights, safety, or security of the website or its users

Any third parties are required to handle data in accordance with data protection law.

---

## 7. International Data Transfers

If personal data is transferred outside the UK, appropriate safeguards will be used, such as:
- UK adequacy regulations
- Standard contractual clauses

---

## 8. Data Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as necessary to:
- Fulfil the purposes described in this policy
- Meet legal or regulatory requirements

When no longer required, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

---

## 9. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Restrict or object to processing
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above.

---

## 10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you have the right to complain to the UK regulator:

**Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)**  
https://ico.org.uk/

We encourage you to contact us first so we can address the issue.

---

## 11. Children’s Data

This website is not intended for children under 11, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so it can be removed.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised date.
